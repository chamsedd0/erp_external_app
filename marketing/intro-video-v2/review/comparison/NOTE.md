# Old vs new: what changed, and why it helps

The side-by-side sheets in this folder pair v1 frames (decoded from `../intro-video/out/*.mp4`, which are unchanged) with v2 frames decoded from the final exports:

- `compare-hero-<fmt>.png` — v1 at 5.0 s against v2 at 3.27 s.
- `compare-interaction-<fmt>.png` — v1 at 7.6 s against v2 at 5.67 s and 7.0 s.
- `compare-odoo-<fmt>.png` — v1 at 14.95 s against v2 at 10.67 s.
- `compare-end-<fmt>.png` — v1 at 17.5 s against v2 at 18.33 s.

## Comprehension

- **One story instead of a feature list.** v1 showed the dashboard, leave, expenses, notifications, four feature cards and Odoo in 19 s. v2 follows one synthetic time-off request: employee submit → Odoo record → manager approval → the employee's Inbox. A buyer can repeat the argument after one viewing: "our people request on mobile, it lands in our Odoo, they see the answer."
- **Odoo is named in the first second.** "Your Odoo. In their hands." opens the film and "Your Odoo. In every pocket." closes it. v1 reached Odoo at 13.8 s, as a compatibility footnote.
- **The Odoo moment is evidence, not a beam.** v1 had a dark invented "Odoo" card and a light wave. v2 shows the request's own record: the same dates, "Paid Time Off", "Family trip", and the Odoo 17 status "To Approve". It is approved by a visible manager click and moves to "Approved". The header re-renders the way Odoo's form does: Approve disappears, Refuse remains.
- **Results are held long enough to read.** v1's toast held about 0.3 s before the next whip. v2 holds the submitted toast 1.7 s, the Odoo record about 2.8 s and the approved Inbox item about 3.4 s.
- **Legible at delivery size.** The UI is shown as flat crops at 2.25–2.6× on a pale studio, and headlines are 112 px (16:9) and 88 px (9:16). Every headline and decisive state reads at 640×360 and 360×640 (`../style-frames/sheet-style-*-preview-size.png`).

## Product truth

- **Removed unsupported claims:** "Book leave in seconds", "Know instantly", "No modules to install", "Odoo 15 → 18", "Multi-company" and "Coming soon to iOS & Android".
- **Neutral CTA.** It is "Meet Shadow Portal." until a real demo destination exists.
- **Real app behaviour, from source:**
  - ISO dates (2026-10-12).
  - The toast text, and no entry animation.
  - The Submit button's 1.05 spring, and its 50 % opacity plus spinner while loading.
  - The Inbox strings and grouping.
- **Nothing approves automatically.** The film never implies that submitting approves, and "Clear status" makes no delivery-time promise.

## Craft

- **The device.** v1 used a CSS phone with a Dynamic-Island cut-out and a flat glare. v2 uses an original generic 3D device:
  - machined graphite frame and a clean screen opening
  - planar-mirror glass with Fresnel
  - softbox and edge light, and a motivated blue rim from the portal light
  - a 120° shutter and thin-lens depth of field on the opening only
- **Three acts through light.** An ink macro → a pale studio for the proof → the brand field. There's no tunnel, particles, glow haze or endless float; the device and UI hold still while you read.
- **Varied compositions.** Macro, whole-device hero, full UI crop, shared record, Inbox detail, lockup. v1 swapped the phone left and right.
- **Continuous transitions.**
  - The device's screen hands off to the flat UI through one push-in, and content dissolves only through the screen's own background.
  - The portal forms around the app and closes onto the real mark.
  - The end card uses the actual lockup, outlined for portability, and holds 180 frames.
- **Sound** is original and restrained, with every interaction cue on its visible cause and the signature at 16.2 s. It is measured only; human listening is still required.
